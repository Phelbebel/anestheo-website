Data Protection

What we store, who can see it, and how it is handled.

What Anestheo holds

  • Clinician account details used to sign in and verify professional status.
  • Patient records a clinician creates or is assigned — including questionnaires, uploaded documents, notes and readiness decisions.

Who can access it

Access is scoped by role and assignment. A clinician sees only their own patients; a patient sees only their own record. This is enforced in the database itself, not just in the interface.

Retention & deletion

Completed or inactive patients can be archived rather than deleted, which keeps the full history (consultations, questions, questionnaires, journey and audit trail) intact and reversible. Permanent deletion is restricted to administrators and is recorded in an audit log.

Your requests

For access, correction or deletion requests relating to your data, contact support@anestheo.com. Full details of how we process personal data are in the Privacy Policy.
