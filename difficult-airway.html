Difficult Airway

Difficult airway algorithm and rescue (DAS-style).

Reference only. Summary for education and quick reference. Not a substitute for clinical judgment, current guidelines, or local protocols. Verify all doses and intervals before use.
Plans — escalation
PlanApproach
Plan AFacemask ventilation & tracheal intubation (optimize: position, blade, bougie, ≤3 attempts)
Plan BSupraglottic airway device (2nd-gen LMA), oxygenate & reassess
Plan CFacemask ventilation — wake patient if feasible
Plan DFront-of-neck access (emergency cricothyroidotomy)
CICO — Can't Intubate Can't Oxygenate
1Declare CICO; call for help and difficult airway trolley.
2Give 100% oxygen; ensure full neuromuscular blockade.
3Final attempt at SAD or facemask if not already done.
4Scalpel–bougie–tube cricothyroidotomy: transverse stab through cricothyroid membrane, rotate blade, slide bougie, railroad 6.0 tube.
5Confirm with capnography; secure and ventilate.
Optimize first attempt
• Position: sniffing / ramped. Pre-oxygenate fully.
• Use a bougie early; external laryngeal manipulation (BURP).
• Limit to 3 + 1 attempts to avoid trauma and CICO spiral.
Important notes
• Call for help early — do not delay declaring CICO.
• Have a pre-formulated, communicated plan before induction in anticipated difficulty.
• Awake fibreoptic / videolaryngoscopy is preferred for predicted severe difficulty.
