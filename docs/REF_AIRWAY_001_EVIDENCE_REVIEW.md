*A source-identification and comparison worksheet to prepare the Airway

---

## How to read this document (please read first)

**This is a preparation worksheet, not a completed clinical review.** It is
written so a qualified clinician can efficiently perform the final review and
sign-off. Specifically:

- **No primary guideline document was retrieved, opened, or quoted** to produce
  this worksheet. The "appropriate source" column **identifies the authoritative
  body/document type** that is the correct place to verify each item. It does
  **not** reproduce their contents.
- **Every "preliminary support" entry is UNVERIFIED.** It reflects commonly
  taught values from general knowledge, offered only as a starting point. It is
  **not** a validation and must not be treated as one. The clinician makes the
  actual determination against the primary document.
- **No source edition, year, page, or quotation is asserted as fact.** Where a
  specific guideline is named, the reviewer must confirm it is the current
  version. Anything marked `[confirm]` is for the reviewer to fill or verify.
- **No reviewer name and no review date have been created.** Those fields are
  left blank for the clinician.
- **No clinical wording in `airway.html` has been changed.** "Recommended
  wording" below is a *proposal for the reviewer to accept, edit, or reject.*

If any statement here reads as if the content were already verified or
evidence-based, that is an error — it is not, and the record stays **Draft**.

**Support codes used below** (all preliminary until the clinician confirms):
`SE` supported exactly · `SM` supported with modification · `NS` not supported ·
`CJ` requires clinician judgment.

## Section-grouped evidence review of the 25 BLOCKER items

Each block gives: the current page wording, the appropriate source, the
**preliminary (unverified)** support code, discrepancies to resolve, and
**proposed** wording for the reviewer to approve.

### Group 1 — Adult tracheal-tube size and depth · A1, A2, A4, A5 · source S5

- **Current:** Adult male ETT **7.5–8.0** mm ID (A1), depth **22–24 cm** (A2);
  adult female ETT **7.0–7.5** mm ID (A4), depth **20–22 cm** (A5).
- **Preliminary support:** **SM** for all four — these ranges are widely taught
  as typical adult starting sizes/depths, but exact values are text- and
  device-dependent and are **estimates**, not patient-specific.
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** no stated definition of "adult male/female"
  (size selection is really by patient anatomy/height, not sex); no statement
  that these are **starting estimates** requiring confirmation (auscultation +
  capnography + depth check).
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** add a leading caveat — *"Typical
  starting sizes for average adults; select by patient anatomy and confirm
  position clinically. Not a substitute for individual assessment."* Keep the
  numbers only if the reviewer confirms them against S5.

### Group 2 — Supraglottic-airway (LMA) sizing · A3, A6, A9, A12, A13–A20 · source S3

- **Current:** adult male LMA **4–5** (A3), adult female LMA **3–4** (A6);
  paediatric "by weight" (A9, A12) pointing to the weight table:
  <5 kg→**1** (A13), 5–10→**1.5** (A14), 10–20→**2** (A15), 20–30→**2.5** (A16),
  30–50→**3** (A17), 50–70→**4** (A18), 70–100→**5** (A19), >100→**5–6** (A20).
- **Preliminary support:** **CJ** — LMA sizing is defined by the **specific
  device's IFU**, and weight bands differ between manufacturers and models. The
  page does not name a device, so no size table can be verified as-is.
- **Discrepancies to resolve (important):**
  1. **Two sizing methods that can conflict.** The fixed adult sizes (A3, A6)
     and the weight table (A18–A20) can disagree for the same adult (e.g., a
     60 kg woman: A6 says 3–4; A18 says 4). The reviewer must choose one
     authoritative method (per the stocked device's IFU) and remove or reconcile
     the other.
  2. **Device not specified.** A13–A20 must be replaced with the weight bands
     from the **actual device's IFU**, or the table must state which device it
     applies to.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** *"Supraglottic airway size per the
  [device name] Instructions for Use (below). Weight bands are device-specific."*
  Then a single, device-sourced table; delete the duplicate fixed-adult row or
  fold it into the weight table.

### Group 3 — Paediatric ETT and depth formulas · A7, A8, A10, A11 · source S4

- **Current:** child cuffed ETT **age/4 + 3.5** (A7), uncuffed **age/4 + 4**
  (A10); depth **age/2 + 12 cm** for both (A8, A11).
- **Preliminary support:** **CJ** — these are recognised approximation formulas,
  but the **cuffed vs uncuffed convention varies by source**, and each is valid
  only within an **age range** (not for neonates/infants, where separate sizing
  applies). No age range is stated on the page.
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** which convention Anestheo endorses; the valid
  age range; behaviour below the formula's range (neonates/infants); that these
  are **estimates** requiring a prepared range of tube sizes.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** *"Approximate sizing for children
  aged [range, per S4]. Have half-sizes above and below available. Different
  references use slightly different formulas; confirm against local paediatric
  guidance. Not for neonates/small infants — see paediatric reference."*

### Group 4 — Difficult-airway prediction thresholds · A22, A23 · sources S1, S2

- **Current:** **3-3-2 rule** — <3 fingers mouth opening, <3 fingers hyoid-chin,
  <2 fingers thyroid-floor of mouth (A22); **thyromental distance <6 cm**
  suggests difficulty (A23).
- **Preliminary support:** **SM** — these bedside tests are described in the
  airway-assessment literature, but they have **limited individual predictive
  value**, which the page does not state. The <6 cm cut-point and the 3-3-2
  finger counts should be confirmed against S1/S2.
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** missing limitation on predictive value; whether
  these are used individually or as part of a multivariable assessment.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** keep the tests, add — *"Bedside
  predictors have limited sensitivity individually; use in combination and
  maintain a difficult-airway plan regardless. Thresholds per [S1/S2]."*

### Group 5 — Preoxygenation target and method · A25 · source S6

- **Current:** preoxygenate **3 min** tidal breathing **or 8 vital-capacity
  breaths**, target **EtO2 >90%** (A25).
- **Preliminary support:** **SM** — commonly cited preoxygenation methods and
  target; confirm against S6 and add that some patients (obesity, pregnancy,
  critical illness) need tailored/longer preoxygenation.
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** patient groups needing modified preoxygenation;
  whether EtO2 monitoring is assumed available.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** *"Preoxygenate to EtO2 >90% where
  measured; 3 min tidal breathing or 8 vital-capacity breaths in cooperative
  patients. Higher-risk patients (obesity, pregnancy, critical illness) may need
  longer or head-up/CPAP techniques."*

### Group 6 — Neuromuscular-blocker doses for RSI · A27 · source S7

- **Current:** **rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg** or **succinylcholine 1–1.5 mg/kg**
  (A27).
- **Preliminary support:** **SM** — both are widely cited RSI doses; confirm the
  exact figures and weight basis against the **SmPC/prescribing information**
  (S7). Missing: **cautions/contraindications** (e.g., suxamethonium
  contraindications; rocuronium reversal availability) and the **weight basis**
  (total vs lean body weight).
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** first-line choice; weight basis; contraindication
  caveat; that this is not individualised advice.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** *"For RSI: rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg or
  suxamethonium 1–1.5 mg/kg [confirm dose and weight basis per SmPC]. Check
  contraindications before use. Maximum/typical doses, not individualised
  prescriptions."*

### Group 7 — Cricoid pressure and capnographic confirmation · A28 · sources S6, S8

- **Current:** *"Apply cricoid pressure per local practice; confirm tube with
  capnography"* (A28).
- **Preliminary support:** cricoid pressure = **CJ** (a **contested** practice;
  guidance/positions vary, and the page already defers to "local practice");
  capnographic confirmation = **SE/SM** (continuous waveform capnography to
  confirm tracheal placement is a **monitoring standard** — confirm exact
  wording against S8).
- **Discrepancies to resolve:** whether to recommend cricoid pressure at all,
  frame it as optional/local, or note the controversy; the capnography statement
  duplicates A29 (Important notes) — deduplicate.
- **Proposed wording (reviewer to approve):** *"Confirm tracheal placement with
  continuous waveform capnography (monitoring standard, S8). Cricoid pressure:
  apply per local protocol; its routine use is debated — follow local
  guidance."*

## Cross-cutting discrepancies (whole-page)

1. **Duplicate/conflicting LMA sizing** (Group 2) — must be reconciled to one
   device-sourced method.
2. **Duplicate capnography statement** — A28 (step 4) and A29 (notes) overlap;
   keep one authoritative statement.
3. **Undefined scope** — adult, paediatric, and obesity content are mixed with
   no population statement; the reviewer decides scope (and whether to split the
   page).
4. **Missing limitations throughout** — device sizes/formulas are estimates;
   bedside predictors have limited predictive value; RSI/cricoid is not
   universal. Each needs an explicit caveat.
5. **Terminology** — "adult male/female" as a sizing key (anatomy/height is the
   real determinant); "beard/large incisors" are more mask-ventilation
   predictors than intubation predictors (reviewer to place correctly).
