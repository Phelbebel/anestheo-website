## 2 · Scope of the page

As displayed, `airway.html` covers:

- **Airway equipment sizing** — tracheal tube (ETT) internal diameter, tube
  depth at the lips, and supraglottic airway (LMA) size, for adults and
  children.
- **Weight-based LMA sizing** — a separate size-by-weight table.
- **Bedside difficult-airway prediction** — Mallampati, the 3-3-2 rule,
  thyromental distance, and a list of anatomical risk factors.
- **A routine preoxygenation / rapid-sequence-induction (RSI) pathway** — with
  positioning, neuromuscular-blocker doses, cricoid pressure, and capnographic
  confirmation.
- **Safety reminders** — capnography confirmation and rescue-device readiness.

**The page does NOT cover** (content absent here; some is linked out to a
separate *Difficult Airway* reference): awake tracheal intubation, extubation,
emergency front-of-neck access (FONA), obstetric airway, or a full
difficult-airway / failed-intubation algorithm. **Population scope is not
stated on the page** and adult, paediatric, and obesity content are mixed
within the same sections.

## 7 · Evidence gaps

- **Unsupported numeric values:** every value in Sections 1 and 2 (all ETT
  sizes, tube depths, LMA sizes and weight bands), the 3-3-2 finger thresholds
  (A22), thyromental < 6 cm (A23), preoxygenation timing/target (A25), and both
  neuromuscular-blocker doses (A27). None carry a source.
- **Unsupported algorithm:** the Section 4 Preoxygenation & RSI pathway
  (A25–A28) is presented as an ordered clinical sequence with no source.
- **Missing source attribution:** the entire page. No source, citation,
  version, publication date, reviewer, or review date exists anywhere for
  airway content in the repository.
- **Inconsistent terminology / scope:** "adult male/female" without any age or
  weight definition; paediatric formulas given for both cuffed and uncuffed
  tubes without stating which to prefer or the valid age range; "ramp obese
  patients" with no obesity threshold; "rapid neuromuscular blocker" named
  generically then given two specific agents.
- **Duplicate / potentially conflicting recommendations:** LMA sizing appears
  **twice** — as fixed adult sizes in Section 1 (male 4–5, female 3–4) and as a
  weight-based table in Section 2 (e.g., 50–70 kg → 4, 70–100 kg → 5, > 100 kg →
  5–6). For a given adult these two methods can disagree, and the page does not
  say which governs. Capnographic confirmation appears twice (A28 step 4 and
  A29), with slightly different wording.
- **Unclear scope:** adult, paediatric, and obesity content are interleaved
  with no per-section population statement; "routine management" is not bounded.
- **Missing limitations:** no statement that device sizes are starting
  estimates; that the paediatric formulas are approximations valid only within
  an age range; that bedside predictors (Mallampati, 3-3-2, thyromental) have
  known limited individual predictive value; or that the RSI/cricoid sequence
  is not universal.
- **Content that appears to combine multiple guidelines / eras:** the
  preoxygenation options (3 min tidal *or* 8 vital-capacity breaths), the RSI
  sequence, and cricoid pressure read as a blend of sources; cricoid pressure
  in particular is a contested practice presented here as a routine step.
- **Content that may represent local practice rather than published evidence:**
  A28 explicitly says cricoid pressure is applied "per local practice"; the
  specific size ranges and depths may reflect a local convention — this cannot
  be determined from the repository.

*Nothing above has been changed. These are observations for the reviewer.*

## 9 · Proposed review scope

- A single clinician review covering **all 30 items (A1–A30)** on
  `airway.html`.
- Before sourcing, the reviewer resolves the **internal conflicts** (the
  two LMA-sizing methods; cuffed-vs-uncuffed paediatric formula selection; the

  obesity content is bounded).

  **explicit limitations**; anything the reviewer cannot source is removed or
  held back, not published.
- The reviewer decides whether to keep one unified page or split adult and
  paediatric content — an editorial decision, recorded but not pre-empted here.
- The signed review is then entered through the existing admin workflow at

## 10 · Questions requiring clinician judgment

1. What are the intended **age and weight definitions** of "adult male",
   "adult female", and each paediatric row?
2. Do the fixed **adult LMA sizes** (A3, A6) govern, or the **weight-based
   table** (A13–A20), when they disagree for the same patient? Which is
   authoritative?
3. Which **paediatric ETT formula** convention does Anestheo endorse — cuffed
   (age/4 + 3.5) or uncuffed (age/4 + 4) — and over what age range are the
   formulas considered valid?
4. Should **cricoid pressure** (A28) be recommended as a routine step, framed
   as optional/local, or removed, given current debate?
5. Is **EtO2 > 90%** (A25) the intended preoxygenation target for all patients,
   and are both timing options (3 min / 8 breaths) endorsed?
6. Is **rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg** or **succinylcholine 1–1.5 mg/kg** (A27) intended
   as first-line, and how should contraindications/cautions be handled?
7. Should device sizes and depths carry an explicit **"starting estimate"**
   caveat, and should the bedside predictors carry a **limited-predictive-value**
   caveat?
8. Is the page's **scope** adult, paediatric, or both — and should it be split?
