# Anestheo Trust Constitution

*A governance document. Not marketing. It governs every public statement Anestheo
makes — on any homepage, product page, calculator, AI feature, clinical reference,
blog post, patient guide, deck, or advertisement — for as long as the company
exists.*

*It sits alongside `FOUNDATION.md` as core governance. The Foundation is why we
exist. This is what we are permitted to say about it.*

*Its purpose is singular: to make it impossible for Anestheo to become — by
ambition, pressure, or drift — a company that overstates evidence, overpromises
capability, or markets beyond reality.*

---

## Article 1 — The First Principle

**We never state more than we can defend with evidence. When evidence and
ambition disagree, evidence wins — every time, without exception.**

Our credibility is the only durable asset we have. Every public sentence either
protects it or spends it. A single caught overstatement, to the audience that
matters most, is not recoverable.

Two corollaries follow, and they are absolute:

- **Silence is always permitted. Overstatement never is.** It is always
  acceptable to say nothing until we can say it truthfully. It is never
  acceptable to say more than is true in order to appear larger, more validated,
  or more mature than we are.
- **When in doubt, we say less.**

---

## Article 2 — The Claims Hierarchy

Every public statement belongs to exactly one of six levels. A statement may be
made only when it meets the evidence required for its level, and it may not be
phrased as if it belonged to a higher one.

1. **Fact** — an objectively verifiable statement about what exists or happened.
   *Evidence: direct verification.* Present tense allowed.
2. **Demonstrated product capability** — something the product actually does,
   reproducibly, in production. *Evidence: it works, reproducibly, and can be
   shown.* Present tense allowed.
3. **Clinical interpretation** — guidance synthesised from clinical sources.
   *Evidence: named source, version, review, stated assumptions and limitations.*
   Present tense allowed **only** when carrying its sourcing; never presented as
   settled truth where clinical uncertainty exists.
4. **Brand belief** — a value statement about how care should be. Must be
   linguistically marked as belief (*"we believe…"*). Never presented as fact.
5. **Vision** — the future we are building toward. Must be future-tense and
   marked (*"we are building toward…"*). Never present tense.
6. **Future ambition** — anything not yet true. Stated only as *"not yet,"*
   *"in development,"* or *"we do not claim this."* Never present tense.

**Moving between levels requires new evidence, never new confidence:**
- A vision or ambition becomes a *capability* only when it is demonstrated in
  production.
- A capability becomes an *outcome claim* only with measured, independent evidence.
- A clinical interpretation becomes *"validated"* or *"evidence-based"* only with
  independent validation, cited.
- Present tense is reserved for Levels 1–3. Levels 4–6 must always be marked as
  belief, future, or not-yet.

Confidence, urgency, funding pressure, and competitive fear are never evidence.

---

## Article 3 — The Evidence Standard

For any public claim, three tiers of evidence exist. The claim's *type* dictates
the tier it requires.

- **Minimum acceptable** — internally verifiable: it is true, and we can show it.
- **Preferred** — documented, sourced, dated, and reproducible; for clinical
  content, named sources plus review.
- **Independent** — verified by a party other than us: external clinical review,
  a security audit, measured outcomes, a certification, or real external adoption.

**Required tier by claim type:**
- Product/existence facts → *minimum*.
- Clinical guidance → *preferred* at minimum; *"validated/proven"* → *independent*.
- Outcomes, risk reduction, efficacy, safety → *independent* only. Never below.
- Security and compliance → *independent* (audit/certification) for any compliance
  claim; *minimum* (architecture verified in production) for factual descriptions.
- Adoption, trust, leadership, "standard" → *independent*, real, and countable.

**When a claim cannot yet be made:** if the required tier is absent, the claim is
not published. It waits. It is not softened into an implication, not moved into
fine print, and not rescued by an adjective. The absence of evidence is stated
plainly or the subject is left unspoken.

## Article 4 — Clinical Integrity

These rules are permanent. They may be strengthened; they may never be relaxed.

1. **Never replace or override clinician judgment.** We support decisions; we do
   not make them.
2. **Never imply certainty where clinical uncertainty exists.** Show the
   uncertainty.
3. **Always distinguish guidance from decisions,** and information from
   determinations.
4. **Show sources whenever practical.** Where a source is not shown, it exists and
   is available.
5. **Admit limitations clearly,** beside the output — never buried, cropped, or
   de-emphasised.
6. **Never hide uncertainty, caveats, or limitations to improve marketing,
   conversion, or appearance.**
7. **Refuse rather than guess.** On missing, out-of-range, or unsupported input, a
   result is withheld.
8. **The clinician is always the responsible decision-maker.** The product is
   always decision support.
9. **Every clinical item carries a source, version, review date, and reviewer.**
   Superseded content is retired and marked, never silently edited.
10. **Founder or internal review is always distinguished from independent
    review,** explicitly, and never described as if it were external validation.

## Article 5 — Product Integrity

Before any feature — calculator, reference, AI feature, dashboard, assessment,
workflow, or patient preparation — is described publicly, it must answer three
questions in plain language:

- **What does it do?** (precise, demonstrable)
- **What does it not do?** (explicit boundaries)
- **Who remains responsible?** (for clinical decisions, always the clinician)

Feature-class rules:

- **Calculators:** sourced values, correct units, stated assumptions and
  limitations, and refusal states. Outputs are labelled as maxima or references —
  never "recommended."
- **References:** sourced, versioned, dated, with the scope of review stated.
- **AI features:** state that they support, never decide; show their basis; never
  present output as a clinical verdict; never imply autonomy, learning from
  patient cases, or accuracy that has not been measured. AI is described by what
  it does, never invoked as a virtue.
- **Dashboards:** neutral groupings unless clinically validated definitions exist;
  any prioritisation rule is visible and reviewed; no severity labels without
  validated definitions; no engagement or attention-manipulating patterns.
- **Assessments:** organise and structure information; never clear a patient,
  never determine risk autonomously. The readiness decision is the clinician's.
- **Patient preparation:** educational and honest; never promises safety, comfort,
  or outcomes.

No feature is ever described beyond what it does in production. "Planned" is
always labelled and never presented as live.

## Article 6 — Marketing Integrity

Anestheo will not use the following words or phrases unless the stated objective
criterion is met. Each criterion is a fact about the world, not a matter of taste.

| Phrase | Permitted only when |
|---|---|
| "Trusted by…" | named, real, countable users have consented to be cited |
| "Clinically proven / proven to…" | measured, methodologically sound evidence exists and is cited |
| "Reduces complications / improves outcomes / safer" | controlled, measured outcome evidence exists |
| "Validated / evidence-based" | independent validation, or the specific evidence, cited |
| "AI-powered / intelligent / smart" | never as praise; AI is described by function, honestly scoped, non-decisional |
| "Seamless / fully integrated" | the integration exists and is verified |
| "Comprehensive / complete / every / all / always" | the statement is literally, universally true |
| "World-class / best / leading" | objective comparative evidence exists (effectively: never) |
| "Standard / the standard" | demonstrated, externally recognised field-wide adoption exists |
| "Revolutionary / transformative / next-generation" | never — unfalsifiable hype, permanently banned |

**The governing habit: describe, do not laud.** Nouns and verbs over adjectives.
If a superlative, an outcome word, or a virtue word is doing the persuading,
delete it and let the fact stand alone.

## Article 7 — Transparency Policy

- **Missing evidence** is stated plainly (*"we do not yet claim…"*), never omitted
  and left to imply.
- **Features under development** are labelled *in development* or *planned*, and
  never shown as live.
- **Known limitations** appear next to the claim or feature they qualify, not in
  fine print.
- **Roadmap** is always separated from current capability — *Available today* /
  *In development* / *Not claimed yet*.
- **Beta functionality** is labelled beta, with its limits stated.

**Why transparency is an advantage, not a weakness.** In medicine, trust is the
scarce resource, and it compounds. Clinicians reward honesty durably and punish
overstatement permanently — a single exposed overclaim ends the relationship.
Admitting a limitation is therefore not a confession; it is a credibility signal
that a competitor who overclaims structurally cannot match. Our honesty about what
we are *not* is the most defensible thing we own. Transparency is the moat.

---

## Article 8 — Trust Review Process

No page, feature, or public claim is published until every applicable gate below is
answered **yes**. Each gate has an owner. There are no exceptions, no "we'll fix it
after launch," and no publishing ahead of a gate. If a gate cannot pass, the claim
is softened or removed until it can.

1. **Clinical review** *(clinical owner)* — every clinical statement sourced,
   reviewed, dated, and attributed; limitations shown; refusal states present.
2. **Evidence review** *(evidence owner)* — every claim mapped to its required
   evidence tier (Article 3); no claim exceeds its evidence; no absolute word
   unconfirmed.
3. **Legal review** *(legal owner)* — regulatory, liability, device, and
   compliance language reviewed.
4. **Security review** *(security/engineering owner)* — every data, access, or
   security claim verified in production.
5. **Founder approval** *(founder)* — final accountability: the founder personally
   affirms the Trust Test (Article 10) for the whole artifact.

---

## Article 9 — Future Authority

Authority is earned, never declared. Each of the following may be communicated only
after its milestone is genuinely met — and each is a status the world grants us
based on evidence, never one we assign ourselves.

- **Adoption** — only after real, counted, consented users or sites exist. Then we
  may state numbers; never before.
- **Outcomes** — only after prospective, measured, methodologically sound (ideally
  independent) data exist. Then we may claim outcomes; never before.
- **Consensus** — only after genuine, disclosed, multi-expert agreement exists.
- **Institutional trust** — only after real, referenceable institutional
  relationships exist, and only with consent.
- **Clinical leadership / "the standard"** — only after sustained external
  recognition and adoption. This is conferred by the field, never claimed by us.

Until each milestone is met, we describe what we are building, and we let the work
earn the words.

---

## Article 10 — The Trust Test

Every sentence Anestheo publishes must survive one permanent question:

> **"If a consultant anesthesiologist with thirty years of experience challenged
> this sentence in public tomorrow, could we defend every word with evidence?"**

If the answer is not an unequivocal **yes**, the sentence is not published. It is
softened, sourced, or removed until the answer is yes.

---

## Amendment

This Constitution may be **strengthened** at any time. It may **never** be weakened
to permit a claim the evidence does not support. Three parts are unamendable: the
**First Principle** (Article 1), **Clinical Integrity** (Article 4), and the
**Trust Test** (Article 10).

Alongside `FOUNDATION.md`, this is a core governance document of Anestheo. It is
written to remain unchanged for the next decade, because the truth does not expire.
