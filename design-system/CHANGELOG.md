# Changelog

The record of what is established, and the rule for how it may change.

---

## Version 1.0 — 2026 · Frozen

The first complete and frozen statement of who Anestheo is and how it looks. Every
item below is settled. They are the constitution, not the legislation — they
change only by the process at the bottom of this document.

**Frozen in 1.0:**

- **Foundation — frozen.** Belief, Vision, Mission, Promise, Values
  (`FOUNDATION.md`). The reason the company exists. Unchanged without founder
  approval; not reopened for a "deeper why."
- **Design philosophy — frozen.** The eight principles (`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`)
  and their derivation from the Foundation.
- **Homepage architecture — frozen.** The six-frame editorial composition and its
  emotional sequence: Belief · Vision · the Work · Promise · Invitation, paper →
  dark → paper. The order and the beats are settled; the craft within them may be
  refined.
- **Typography — frozen.** Newsreader (display) and Inter (system); two faces,
  never three. The scale, the rule of two, the measure (`TYPOGRAPHY.md`).
- **Color philosophy — frozen.** Paper, the dark workspace, one teal at the law of
  scarcity, the ban on decorative gradients and a second accent (`COLOR.md`).
- **The Point — introduced and frozen as a primitive.** Presence, kept still. Its
  philosophy (`POINT.md`) and its implementation (`point.css`, imported by
  `styles.css`). Inherited by every dark composition via `class="lit"`. Never
  animated, never the focal centre, warm not blue, subtle to the edge of being
  missed.
- **Motion philosophy — frozen.** One easing, one duration, almost never; motion
  only where it reinforces meaning; reduced-motion parity (`MOTION.md`).
- **Copy philosophy — frozen.** The voice — plain, certain, human, exact, quiet —
  and the banned words (`COPY.md`).
- **Governance — established.** `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` (what we rejected and why),
  `NORTH_STAR.md` (the review gate), and this changelog.

**Built and verified against 1.0:**

- The homepage (`index.html`) — the language made real.
- The Point primitive (`point.css`) — available to every workspace.
- The Patient Journey loop closed end to end (a product milestone, recorded here
  for the timeline; its rationale lives with the product, not the brand).

## How this may change

> **Evolution is encouraged. Redefinition is not.**

Two kinds of change exist, and they are not equal.

**Evolution — encouraged, no approval required.**
Work that expresses the frozen system more faithfully. New pages, products, and
workspaces generated from the language. Refinement of craft within a frozen
architecture — a better line break, a cleaner component, a sharper sentence — so
long as every change still passes `NORTH_STAR.md`. This is the daily work. Do it
freely and often.

**Breaking changes — require explicit founder approval.**
Anything that alters the constitution rather than expressing it. For example:

- changing any line of the Foundation;
- adding a third typeface, a second accent color, or a new gradient;
- making the Point animate, or move it to the centre, or brighten it to be seen;
- reviving anything in `DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`;
- changing the homepage's six-frame architecture or emotional sequence;
- weakening any question in `NORTH_STAR.md`.

These are not design choices. They are amendments. They do not happen in a pull
request or a design review; they happen only with the founder's explicit
approval, and they are recorded here as a new version.

**The rule of thumb:** if a change makes the work express the system better, it is
evolution — ship it. If it changes what the system *is*, it is an amendment —
stop and ask.
