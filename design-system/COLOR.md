# Color

Color in Anestheo is almost absent — and that is the point. We work in a warm,
near-monochrome world so that the rare moment of color *means* something. Color
is a signal, never a decoration. Scarcity is what makes it valuable.

There are only two worlds: **paper** (where the company speaks) and the **dark
workspace** (where the work happens). The whole system lives between them.

---

## The paper world

The dominant surface. Warm, light, editorial — the page of a fine publication.
We chose light over dark as the company's voice because dark reads as "tech
startup," and light reads as timeless and humble. Medicine requires humility.

| Token | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FAF8F4` | Warm off-white. Not pure white — pure white is clinical and cold; warmth is human. |
| Ink | `#15211C` | A near-black with a green undertone. Softer than `#000`, and quietly tied to the brand. |
| Muted | `#5C665F` | Secondary text. Present, not shouting. |
| Faint | `#8A938C` | Labels, the quietest type. |
| Hairline | `rgba(21,33,28,.10–.12)` | Our primary divider. A line, not a box. |

## The dark workspace

Where the product lives. The work happens in the dark — focused, watched over.

| Token | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dark | `#070D0A` – `#0A1410` | A deep, near-black green-black. The room the work happens in. |
| On-dark | `#F4F2EC` | Warm white text — never pure white. The dark world stays warm too. |
| On-dark muted | `rgba(244,242,236,.55)` | Secondary text on dark. |
| Dark hairline | `rgba(244,242,236,.12–.14)` | The divider, in the dark. |
| The Point | `#F7EFDD` | The single warm light. Its own law lives in `POINT.md`. |

The dark world is never lit evenly. It is lit *from the Point*. See `POINT.md`.

## The accent

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Teal | `#1B6B5A` |

One color. **The rule of scarcity: teal appears at most twice on a page.** On the
homepage, it is the two door arrows — and nowhere else. Color withheld for an
entire page and then spent on two small marks is unforgettable; color sprinkled
everywhere is wallpaper.

**Why so little.** An accent earns its meaning from rarity. The moment teal
appears on three things, it stops being a signal and becomes a theme — and a
theme is decoration (Principle 4). The discipline is the brand.

---

## When color is forbidden

- **To create hierarchy.** That is typography's job (`TYPOGRAPHY.md`). Never color
  a word to make it important.
- **To decorate.** No gradients-as-style, no glows, no tinted panels for variety.
- **On the monuments.** The Belief and the Promise are pure ink on paper. Color
  on a monument cheapens it.
- **As a third hue.** There is paper, there is dark, there is one teal. A second
  accent does not exist. (Status colors inside the live product — the reds, ambers,
  greens of clinical urgency — are functional product UI, governed by clarity and
  safety, not by this brand palette. Keep the two separate in your mind.)

---

## Gradients

Decorative gradients are banned. The only gradient that exists is **functional
light** — the dim between the paper world and the dark world, and the wash a
composition receives *from the Point*. Both describe light falling; neither is
ornament. If a gradient is not describing light, delete it.

The test for any color decision: *remove it. If the page is calmer and still
clear, it should not have been there.*
