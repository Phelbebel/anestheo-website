# North Star — the review every screen must pass

Before anything ships — a page, a component, a workflow, an email, a slide — it
passes this review. These are not suggestions. They are a gate. **Every question
must be answered YES.** A single NO means the work is not finished; it is returned,
not shipped.

The review is short on purpose. If a design cannot pass ten plain questions, it
will not survive a decade.

How to use it: hold the work in front of you and answer honestly, out loud if you
can. "Mostly" is a NO. "It's fine" is a NO. Only a clear yes is a yes.

---

### 1 · Does this clearly express `FOUNDATION.md`?
Trace it to a line — Belief, Vision, Mission, Promise, or a Value. If you cannot
name the line it expresses, it does not belong to Anestheo. This is the first
question because it is the only one that cannot be compensated for.

### 2 · Can something still be removed?
Try. Remove an element, a word, a color, a rule. If the work is not visibly worse,
it stays removed and you ask again. You are done removing only when the next
removal would do harm. *Remove before adding.*

### 3 · Does typography lead before interface?
Cover the controls. Is the page still clear from type, scale, and space alone? If
the hierarchy collapses without buttons, boxes, or color, the typography was not
doing its job. *Typography creates hierarchy.*

### 4 · Is the product still the proof?
Where we show the work, is it real, whole, legible, and shown with dignity — never
a mockup, never fake, never a feature wall? The product is evidence the Foundation
is true. *Product is always the proof.*

### 5 · Would this still feel correct in 2045?
Name anything that is here because it is current. A trend, an effect, a fashion.
If you find one, it will date. What remains — type, space, alignment, restraint —
is what lasts.

### 6 · Does it embody Quiet Authority?
Read the tone. Is it certain without being loud? Does it trust the reader's
intelligence? If any part is shouting, performing, or anxious, it is not us.

### 7 · Does it respect restraint?
Count the accents, the typefaces, the motions, the colors. Two typefaces. One
teal, used at most twice. One easing. Almost no motion. If the count has crept up,
the discipline has slipped.

### 8 · Is motion meaningful rather than decorative?
For every animation, state the meaning it reinforces. If the answer is "it looks
nice," remove it. If a visitor would notice it, it is too much. The Point never
moves at all.

### 9 · Would Apple, Leica, or Linear remove something instead of adding something?
This is the temperament test. The companies we measure against reach for less.
When you felt the urge to add — a flourish, a section, a color — would they have
subtracted instead? Do what they would do.

### 10 · Would this still feel unmistakably Anestheo if the logo disappeared?
Cover the wordmark. Is it still ours — by its restraint, its voice, its
composition, its steady light? A brand that needs its logo to be recognized has
no design language. We have one; this question proves it.

---

## The shorthand

When there is no time for ten, three of them carry the rest:

1. Does it express the Foundation?
2. Could you remove something and make it stronger?
3. Will it look correct in 2045?

Three yeses are necessary but not sufficient. Ten yeses is the bar.

---

This checklist does not evolve casually. It is part of the frozen system
(`CHANGELOG.md`). The work changes to pass the review; the review does not change
to pass the work.
