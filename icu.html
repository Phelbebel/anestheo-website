Sedation & analgesia targets
• Target light sedation (RASS 0 to −2) unless contraindicated.
• Daily sedation interruption / spontaneous awakening trials reduce ventilator days.
• Assess pain (CPOT/BPS) and delirium (CAM-ICU) routinely.
Important notes
• Predicted body weight drives the tidal volume — not actual weight.
• Escalate to noradrenaline early in septic shock after fluid resuscitation; target MAP ≥ 65 mmHg.
