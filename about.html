About Anestheo

Our mission

Anestheo is a platform of educational anesthesia resources and clinical reference tools, built for patients preparing for surgery and for the anesthesiologists, residents, ICU physicians and perioperative teams who care for them.

For patients

We help patients understand their anesthesia before surgery through clear, accessible educational material and a simple pre-operative questionnaire that their clinician can review in advance.

For clinicians

Anestheo brings patient management, secure pre-operative questionnaires, and a suite of bedside reference tools — drug dosing, airway, regional, TIVA/TCI and sedation, emergency protocols and clinical scores — into one workspace.

Reference, not advice

All tools and content are provided for information and education only. They support, but never replace, the professional judgement of a qualified clinician.
