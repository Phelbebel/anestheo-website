Cookie Policy

How Anestheo uses cookies and local browser storage.

What we use

Anestheo relies on a small amount of browser storage to work. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

  • Essential (authentication & session) — keeps you signed in. Your authentication token is stored by our sign-in provider so you don't have to log in on every page.
  • Preferences — remembers lightweight interface state (for example, which panels you last opened) on your own device.

Managing storage

You can clear this storage at any time through your browser settings. Clearing the authentication entry signs you out. Because the essential entries are required for the application to function, disabling them will prevent you from logging in.

More detail

Cookie use is covered alongside our broader data practices in the Privacy Policy and Data Protection pages. Questions? Contact support.

This policy describes current behaviour and may be updated as the product evolves.
