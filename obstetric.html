Management of hypotension after spinal
1Left uterine displacement / wedge.
2Phenylephrine infusion or boluses (first-line pressor).
3IV fluid co-load.
4Treat bradycardia with ephedrine or glycopyrrolate / atropine.
Important notes
• Always use left uterine displacement to avoid aortocaval compression.
• Anticipate difficult airway — have smaller tubes and a video laryngoscope ready.
• For high-risk hemorrhage, ensure large-bore access and blood availability.
