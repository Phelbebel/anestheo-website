Nobody should have to be lucky to receive good anesthetic care.

Good anesthetic care depends on preparation, knowledge, and judgment being available when they’re needed. We’re building tools that make that easier for the clinician and clearer for the patient, while keeping the decision where it belongs: with the clinician.

Questions.

Is Anestheo a medical device?↓

We do not currently claim medical-device status or regulatory clearance. Anestheo provides decision support and educational tools; the clinician makes the clinical decision.

Does it replace clinical judgment?↓

No. Anestheo supports decisions; it does not make them. The responsible clinician remains responsible.

Who writes and reviews the content?↓

Content is currently selected, synthesised, and reviewed by our founder. We do not claim independent review.

How is patient information protected, and are you HIPAA or GDPR compliant?↓

Anestheo uses authenticated, role-based access designed so a clinician sees a patient only when assigned. We do not currently claim HIPAA or GDPR compliance; our privacy page states what is and isn’t in place.

Who is responsible for decisions made using the platform?↓

The responsible clinician is responsible for clinical decisions; Anestheo is a support tool.
