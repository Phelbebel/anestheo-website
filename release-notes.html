Release Notes

A running record of notable changes to Anestheo.

Recent highlights

  • A redesigned clinician Home that opens on a "Today's Overview" panel and a Patient Inbox of the work that needs you.
  • Safe patient archiving — archive completed or inactive patients without losing any history, with restore and an audit trail.
  • Inline clinical reference and resource libraries that expand in place, without leaving the page.
  • Evidence transparency for clinical tools, with a permanent evidence identity and a documented review lifecycle.
