Assessment — predictors of difficulty
• Mallampati III–IV — limited view of posterior pharynx.
• 3-3-2 rule — <3 fingers mouth opening, <3 fingers hyoid-chin, <2 fingers thyroid-floor of mouth.
• Thyromental distance <6 cm suggests difficulty.
• Reduced neck mobility, large incisors, receding mandible, beard, obesity.
Preoxygenation & RSI
1Preoxygenate 3 min tidal breathing or 8 vital-capacity breaths (target EtO2 >90%).
2Position: sniffing position; ramp obese patients (ear to sternal notch).
3RSI: induction agent + rapid neuromuscular blocker (rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg or succinylcholine 1–1.5 mg/kg).
4Apply cricoid pressure per local practice; confirm tube with capnography.
Important notes
• Always confirm placement with continuous waveform capnography.
• Have a backup plan and rescue devices (LMA, bougie, video laryngoscope) immediately available.
• For anticipated difficulty, see the Difficult Airway reference.
