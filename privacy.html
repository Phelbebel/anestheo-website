Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what information Anestheo collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Anestheo you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email and role you provide when creating an account.
  • Clinical information — pre-operative questionnaire responses and related details you or your patients submit.
  • Usage information — basic technical data needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To provide patient management, questionnaires and clinical reference tools.
  • To authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • To communicate service updates you have asked to receive.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Clinical information is accessible only to the patient and the clinician(s) involved in their care. We use trusted infrastructure providers to host data under appropriate safeguards.

Data security & retention

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us. Depending on your location, additional rights may apply.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@anestheo.com.
