Safe practice
1Calculate the maximum safe dose by weight before drawing up.
2Use ultrasound guidance; visualize needle tip and spread.
3Aspirate before and during injection; inject incrementally (3–5 mL).
4Monitor for LAST during and after injection — see the LAST reference.
Important notes
• Reduce doses in elderly, cachectic, hepatic or cardiac impairment.
• Add epinephrine 1:200,000 as a marker of intravascular injection and to extend duration.
• Lipid emulsion (Intralipid 20%) must be immediately available — see LAST.
